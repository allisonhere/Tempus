## Tempus UI

Tempus is a full interface addon for **Warcraft Forever**. Unit frames, nameplates, party and raid frames, buffs, action bars, bags, chat, a square minimap and skins for Blizzard's windows all share one look, and every part is a module you can switch off. Use all of it, or keep only what you like.

Forever hides health, auras and range from addons during combat. Tempus is written around that, so your bars, auras and colours keep updating in the middle of a fight.

### Fighting

- **Unit, party and raid frames.** Cast bars, portraits, healing and absorb cues, dispel tints, range fading and aggro borders. Click casting is built in, with starting bindings for Druid, Priest, Shaman and Paladin. Combo points rise in height and shade from red to green as you build them.
- **Nameplates** that show threat, interruptible casts (with your own interrupt's cooldown), quest progress, a level and danger strip, and the buffs you can dispel or steal.
- **Buffs and debuffs** with timers, a watch list for buffs you never want to forget, and expiry warnings.
- **Combat Pulse**, one compact strip with your DPS, a swing line, and small cues that appear only when they matter: an interruptible cast, a buff to purge, threat trouble, the execute range.
- **Swing timer, resource ticker, cooldown row and danger alerts** for auto attacks, energy and mana ticks, spells you pick, and elites or crowds you should notice. Under the tick bar, a strip lights up when you can cast Claw, Shred or your own picks, and small dots time your bleeds and buffs. Both can be moved.
- **Cooldown and debuff timelines.** Show your cooldowns, or your own debuffs on the target, as icons that fall down a themed timeline toward the moment they are ready or run out. Background, fade, columns and size are adjustable, with a live preview in settings.
- **Action bars** with fading, hotkeys and range colouring.

### Bags, bank and auction house

- **Combined bags and bank** with instant search, filters for gear, consumables, trades, quests and junk, and custom categories. Item level and upgrade marks show on gear. The bank has its own row of tabs, including the ones you can still buy, with prices in the tooltips. You can also look at what your other characters carry, even offline.
- **Auction house.** Tempus scans the auction house itself and shows the lowest buyout on item tooltips. **Gear Advisor**, a list of the best gear deals for your class and level, uses that saved scan, so it works anywhere. It follows your auction house filters (category, rarity, level range), has a maximum price, and a detail pane compares the item you wear with the one you would buy, stat by stat. Pick the build you play (every class has its builds; Druids can follow their shapeshift form) and it scores gear for that build, says why, and labels its scores as estimates. Listings no better than gear in your bags are left out, and `/tempus gearplan` suggests the best set to buy for an amount of gold.
- **Stat tooltips** that explain what an item's stats do for your character, such as attack power, crit, health and armor.
- **Shopping list** for items you are collecting, with what you and your alts already have.
- **Vendor and quest helpers** that sell grey items, repair, and handle routine quest turn-ins.

### Look and feel

- **Chat** you can select and copy, history that survives a reload, whisper alerts, clickable links and fading tabs. If [SpellPower](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spellpower) is installed, its spellcheck and grammar options appear in Tempus's Chat settings.
- **A square minimap**, XP and reputation bars, and icons for ore and herb nodes you have gathered. The info bar has an optional **Movement speed** slot: see your percent increase over normal running speed, with current speed in km/h or mph beside it. Pick the slot and units under Minimap > Info Bar.
- **Flight timer** with the animated Tempus hourglass. Your first trip shows “Learning route” and counts up; after landing, Tempus remembers the duration and counts down on later trips along that route, averaging the times as you fly. The compact bar sits above zone announcements. Unlock the UI to move it, and adjust its size under Convenience > Flight timer. The hourglass stays still when Animations is off.
- **Waypoints.** `/way 52.3 41.8 Bank` sets a destination in your current zone. A compact compass at the top of the screen has a white degree dial, shaded sides, a metal rim, a red center pointer and a gold destination diamond. In Waypoints settings you can switch it to a flat dark tape or a minimal marks-only look. It shows straight-line direction and distance in yards, with gold pins on the minimap and world map. Distant minimap pins stay at the edge; open the destination’s zone on the world map to see its pin. Click the x or use `/way clear` to remove the destination. Use `/way #mapID x y Label` for another map, or `/tway` if another addon uses `/way`. Unlock the UI to move the compass; destinations survive a reload.
- **Skins** for Blizzard windows, tooltips and chat, and for Bagnon and DBM.
- **Style Studio** to preview themes, fonts, bars, colours and icons together, with the accent colour applied everywhere.
- **Movable everything**, with saved layouts, automatic layouts for solo, party and raid, and a settings search.

Type `/tempus` to open settings. A first-run setup helps you pick modules, UI scale and a layout.
